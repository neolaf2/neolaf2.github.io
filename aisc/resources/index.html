Refresh policy

IEEE is authoritative for formal status. This edition retains the report’s dated status snapshot because live standards and PAR catalog retrieval could not be verified. Future updates should compare source records, review differences and rebuild the site. A failed retrieval never overwrites verified content.

Portfolio groupings and four-level placements are editorial aids. An approved PAR is not a published standard. Proposed initiatives are not presented as adopted IEEE requirements.
